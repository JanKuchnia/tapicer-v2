## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + Tailwind CSS / Vanilla CSS (standalone, zero-build deployment, fast TTFB, maximum performance and local SEO focus).

## Users

- **Primary users**: Classic car collectors, vintage vehicle owners, and high-end automotive enthusiasts in Kraków, Myślenice, and throughout Małopolska seeking bespoke automotive leatherwork, authentic period-correct interior restoration, and rare material sourcing (e.g., original pattern books, Connolly-grade leather, Alcantara).
- **Secondary users**: Discerning modern vehicle owners needing premium repairs or personalization: steering wheel re-trimming (smooth/perforated leather, custom stitch, Alcantara), motorcycle seats, convertible tops, and dashboards.
- **Tertiary users**: Owners of horse-drawn carriages (karety, bryczki) and antique furniture requiring traditional saddlery (*rymarstwo*) techniques.

## Product Purpose

Establish trust and convey master-craftsman authority for Marcin Nowak's atelier. The website guides prospective clients through the workshop's heritage, capabilities, and past projects, driving qualified local inquiries directly via phone (`605 304 999`) for in-person consultation and bespoke quotes.

## Positioning

Artisanal leather craftsmanship rooted in a family saddlery tradition since 1998, with an independent workshop established in 2004. Unlike high-volume auto upholstery repair shops, the atelier combines traditional equestrian saddlery techniques with bespoke vintage automotive restoration. Every project is executed by hand on an individual appointment basis; the business does not compete on speed or mass-market pricing.

## Operating Context

- Workshop location: Krzyszkowice 320A, 32-445 Krzyszkowice (near Zakopianka, between Kraków and Myślenice).
- Workshop hours: Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00; Saturday–Sunday closed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities**: Full automotive leather interiors, historic classic car interior restoration, leather and Alcantara steering wheel re-covering, headliners and pillars, convertible soft tops, dashboards, small interior elements (gear knobs, gaiters, handbrake sleeves, armrests), motorcycle seats, horse-drawn vehicle upholstery, and antique furniture restoration.
- **Constraints**: No fixed price list published online. No claims of "szybko", "ekspresowo", or immediate turnaround (appointments booked in advance). Polish language only.
- **Open / Undecided**: Official business email address, NIP/REGON display, and official social media profile URLs (currently unconfirmed; omitted from customer-facing copy until verified).

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Speed**: Meticulous handwork and durability take precedence over volume; expectations about queues and appointment-based scheduling are transparently stated.
2. **Heritage as the Differentiator**: The lineage from equestrian saddlery to vintage automobile interior restoration is the unique, irreproducible foundation of trust.
3. **Proof Precedes Claims**: Capabilities are anchored in real photographic evidence, material details, and documented project histories rather than marketing superlatives.
4. **Frictionless Local Contact**: Direct phone contact (`605 304 999`) and clear workshop location/hours are accessible immediately across all screen sizes.
