## Rules for every piece

- Original photos only. No stock car interiors.
- Write in the client's voice: personal, craft-proud, formal-polite (see Tone signals in `research.md`).
- No prices, no turnaround promises.
- Ask the client to read every piece before it goes live (facts about their own work).
